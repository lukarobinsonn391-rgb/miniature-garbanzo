<h2><strong>What You Pay for When You Form an LLC</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Every LLC pays a state filing fee. Two private fee compilations checked in October 2026 show fees from $35 in Montana to $500 in Massachusetts, and many states add a yearly report or tax.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The SBA says the total cost to register a business "will be less than $300" while noting that fees vary by state (SBA, page last modified August 28, 2026).</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">A service fee sits on top of that. Other costs to watch:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Registered agent:</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Every state requires one. You can act as your own or pay for a service.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>EIN:</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> The IRS says, "You never have to pay a fee for an EIN" (IRS, page updated October 16, 2026). Some services still charge for it.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Operating agreement:</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> The SBA recommends one even where the state doesn't require it.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Federal ownership report:</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> FinCEN's final rule, issued August 11, 2026, exempts domestic companies from beneficial ownership information reporting.</span></li>
</ul>
